NERIS Data Export
=================


Files
=================

[incidents.parquet]
Type: Parquet


The base incident-level model for the incident basics' incidents layer.


Field Name                 | Alias                      | Definition
---------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------------------------------------
neris_id_incident          | Incident NERIS ID          | The unique identifier in NERIS for the incident.
neris_id_dept              | Department NERIS ID        | The unique identifier for the agency in NERIS.
department_name            | Department Name            | The name of the department.
department_state           | Department State           | The state where the department's primary headquarters is located.
type_1                     | Type 1                     | The disposition or final incident type as assessed on scene. Medical incident types have been generalized.
type_1_primary             | Type 1 Primary             | Whether type_1 was the condition that best defines the overall incident.
type_2                     | Type 2                     | The disposition or final incident type as assessed on scene. Medical incident types have been generalized.
type_3                     | Type 3                     | The disposition or final incident type as assessed on scene. Medical incident types have been generalized.
special_modifiers          | Special Modifiers          | Incident modifier(s) that can help describe the magnitude or category of the incident.
aid_direction              | Aid Direction              | Whether aid was given or received by the department for the incident.
nonfd_aid                  | NonFD Aid                  | Comma-separated list of types of non-fire department support agencies from which aid was received.
unit_response_count        | Unit Response Count        | The number of units dispatched to the incident. Values over 1,000,000 are removed.
rescue_animal              | Rescue Animal              | The number of animals rescued. Values over 1,000,000 are removed.
call_create                | Call Create                | The timestamp when the call was created and call processing began in the Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) or dispatch center.
duration                   | Duration                   | The time in seconds between call_create and when dispatch closed the incident. Values over 1,000,000 are removed.
civic_location             | Civic Location             | A concatenation of the elements of the incident's civic location. Generally an address or intersection.
incident_state             | Incident State             | The state where the incident occurred.
incident_county            | Incident County            | The county where the incident occurred.
type_location_use          | Location Use               | The primary use type of the location.
in_use                     | In Use                     | Whether the location was in use.
intended                   | Intended Use               | Whether the location was being used as intended.
type_vacancy               | Vacancy Type               | If not in use, apparent reason the location was not in use.
type_location_use__second  | Secondary Location Use     | Secondary location use type, if there was one that impacted the incident response.
acres_burned               | Acres Burned               | Estimated number of acres burned during the incident. Values over 100,000,000 are removed.
room_of_origin             | Room of Origin             | Room of origin of the fire.
building_damage            | Building Damage            | Rating of damage to the fire building of origin.
action_and_tactics         | Actions and Tactics        | The actions and/or tactics taken by the department at the scene of the incident. Medical actions and tactics have been generalized.
exposure_count             | Exposure Count             | The number of structures, vehicles, and other property affected by the incident that were not the primary incident location. Values over 1,000,000 are removed.
displacement_count         | Displacement Count         | The number of people and businesses displaced due to the incident. Values over 1,000,000 are removed.
displacement_causes        | Displacement Causes        | The cause of displacement for the incident.
smoke_alarm_present        | Smoke Alarm Present        | Whether a smoke alarm was present at the incident location.
smoke_alarm_types          | Smoke Alarm Types          | The types of smoke alarms present at the incident location.
smoke_alarm_operation      | Smoke Alarm Operation      | The determination of if and how the alarm operated during the incident.
fire_suppression_present   | Fire Suppression Present   | Whether there was a fire suppression system (e.g., sprinkler system) present at the incident location.
fire_suppression_types     | Fire Suppression Types     | The types of fire suppression systems present at the incident location.
fire_suppression_operation | Fire Suppression Operation | The determination of whether the fire suppression system operated as designed, intended, or expected.
csst_hazard_flag           | CSST Hazard Flag           | Whether CSST was identified as the ignition source in the incident.
electric_hazard_flag       | Electric Hazard Flag       | Whether one or more electric hazards were identified as part of the incident.
powergen_hazard_flag       | Powergen Hazard Flag       | Whether there was a power generation hazard at the incident location.
medical_oxygen_hazard_flag | Medical Oxygen Hazard Flag | Whether there was a medical oxygen present at the incident location.
point_origin               | Point Origin               | Where the point associated with the incident came from in the incident data structure.
x                          | x                          | The x-coordinate of the point associated with the incident.
y                          | y                          | The y-coordinate of the point associated with the incident.
export_date                | Export Date                | The date and time when the incident record was exported from the NERIS system.
